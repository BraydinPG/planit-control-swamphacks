01 / ENVIRONMENTAL INTELLIGENCE

National Weather Service

NWS adapters retrieve weather observations, hourly forecasts and active warnings. Readings retain their source, location, timestamps and state before they become site context.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYAn issued warning is distinct from an on-site observation or a supervisor-confirmed active event.

01 / ENVIRONMENTAL INTELLIGENCE

NOAA

Best-effort GOES GLM supplies total-lightning detections. NOAA historical event records separately support research into recorded-event duration.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYSatellite detection has coverage, resolution and latency limits. Historical archives are not current site observations.

01 / ENVIRONMENTAL INTELLIGENCE

National Hurricane Center

The tropical adapter collects NHC storm information and related NWS tropical watches and warnings as geographically qualified regional context.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYA storm center is not a measured wind field at a worksite. Historical best tracks are post-analysis, not issued operational forecasts.

01 / ENVIRONMENTAL INTELLIGENCE

USGS

Earthquake and stream-gauge adapters add event locations, origin times and gauge readings. Their values and geographic relationships remain source-specific.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYAn earthquake magnitude is not local equipment damage. Gauge height without a verified relationship is not site inundation depth.

EIA reference locations

Validated EIA-860M power-plant coordinates provide representative infrastructure locations for the map and site inventory.

Crew field evidence

Authorized crew members can submit a photo, category, location, present-or-absent answer and optional note. The report preserves the source of field evidence.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYA submission or Gemini classification does not automatically become a confirmed hazard or an approved instruction.

02 / DATA PROCESSING

Geographic matching

Adapters and site loaders use coordinates, distances and source-specific coverage to relate regional evidence to a location. Event and station coordinates remain part of the record.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYRegional proximity is not a verified on-site measurement. Unsupported site associations must remain explicit.
Inspect source reference ↗

02 / DATA PROCESSING

Time & source checks

Observation, valid and retrieval times identify when evidence applies and when it was fetched. Source identifiers and quality states travel with normalized readings.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYA recent retrieval cannot make an old observation current. Historical source revisions do not establish original issue-time availability.
Inspect source reference ↗

02 / DATA PROCESSING

Data normalization

Provider adapters translate their source formats into the shared Observation contract, retaining units, location, hazard type and availability state.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYMissing source values stay missing. A failed request must not become a zero reading or a successful no-hazard result.
Inspect source reference ↗

02 / DATA PROCESSING

Caching & deduplication

Source-specific caches and shared-resource retrieval reduce duplicate upstream work. Bounded fetching feeds a serial persistence and scoring pipeline.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYCached evidence ages. The controlled request-reduction benchmark does not establish a production latency guarantee.
Inspect source reference ↗

02 / DATA PROCESSING

Current / stale / unavailable

Each feed tracks its own freshness. A failed refresh can return its last readings as stale, then unavailable once the configured age limit is exceeded.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYOne healthy provider cannot make another current. Missing evidence cannot silently clear an existing deterministic alert.
Inspect source reference ↗

02 / DATA PROCESSING

Tiger Data persistence

The refresh and report services store observations, scores, alerts and review history against sites, teams and organizations. Site APIs load that authoritative state.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYThe public diagram does not query the database. Real reads and writes require the configured service and appropriate authorization.

03 / ANALYTICAL SYSTEMS

Deterministic hazard index

The approved rules-0.2 algorithm combines eligible current factors and confirmed-present reports into an explainable 0–10 site index. The backend stores the result and factor breakdown.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYML persistence estimates and Gemini output never contribute to the score. Missing evidence is not verified low risk.
Inspect source reference ↗

03 / ANALYTICAL SYSTEMS

Lightning safety rules

A qualifying strike within ten miles triggers the deterministic stop-work rule. A new qualifying strike resets the thirty-minute watch window.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYAcknowledgement and the countdown do not themselves authorize work. A feed outage cannot be treated as proof the condition cleared.
Inspect source reference ↗

03 / ANALYTICAL SYSTEMS

Event-duration research

The multi-event adapter dispatches qualified duration targets separately. It requires trusted active-event confirmation, a usable start time and an eligible site relationship.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYUnsupported events, missing artifacts, missing starts or insufficient evidence produce unavailable advisories. Recorded event end is not worksite clearance.
Inspect source reference ↗

03 / ANALYTICAL SYSTEMS

Survival & persistence

Kaplan–Meier baselines and regularized logistic survival models estimate persistence conditional on an already-active event. Estimator selection and horizons follow target-specific retrospective evaluation.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYThese are recorded-event probabilities, not event-onset, injury or local severity probabilities. Research limitations remain attached.
Inspect source reference ↗

03 / ANALYTICAL SYSTEMS

Offline Monte Carlo

Fixed-seed draws check consistency with a model-implied distribution and support offline sensitivity and conditional research scenarios. The Data page presents precomputed research.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYMonte Carlo does not execute for every live request or in this explorer. Sampling agreement is separate from held-out predictive skill.
Inspect source reference ↗

Gemini evidence summaries

Gemini receives supplied evidence to produce structured summaries and suggestions. Validation, references and fallback handling keep that output reviewable.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYGemini cannot confirm hazards, set or explain the deterministic score, clear alerts or authorize work.

04 / DECISION SUPPORT

Structured hazard reports

Reports retain photo evidence, location, category and present-or-absent state. Present reports can produce potential notices; absent reports follow their own organization review.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYA potential notice has no approved instruction. Suggested classifications remain suggestions until human review.

04 / DECISION SUPPORT

Human-reviewed instructions

A notified supervisor confirms the hazard for their organization, reviews or edits a suggested instruction and sends the final text to that organization’s teams inside the radius.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYOne organization’s confirmation does not authorize another organization. Guests and crew cannot perform supervisor review.
Inspect source reference ↗

04 / DECISION SUPPORT

Scheduled crew briefings

At 6 PM site-local time, the scheduler generates and stores a next-workday briefing for eligible assigned-team sites whose organization has a supervisor.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYBriefings depend on the configured scheduler and evidence. They are advisory records, not a manual safety-clearance action.

04 / DECISION SUPPORT

Organizational permissions

Auth0 identity is validated by FastAPI. Backend roles, organization ownership and membership, and team assignments determine access. Ownership is stored in the database; each organization has at most one supervisor.

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARYFrontend role guards improve navigation; server-side permission checks remain authoritative.

FROM INFORMATION TO ACTION

ONE SHARED PICTURE.
ACCOUNTABLE DECISIONS.

Observations, reports, alerts and predictions carry different meanings. FORESITE keeps their sources and responsibilities visible as information reaches the field.

  1. 01

    Live observations

    Source adapters collect environmental readings and issued notices. Crews supply field evidence.

  2. 02

    Site evidence

    Location, source and time qualify each reading. Missing or stale evidence keeps its own state.

  3. 03

    Hazard evaluation

    Deterministic rules evaluate current factors. Eligible event-duration outlooks remain separate.

  4. 04

    Crew awareness

    Authorized crews see relevant alerts, notices and observations together, with the evidence behind them.

  5. 05

    Supervisor review

    The notified organization's supervisor confirms a hazard and reviews any suggested instruction.

  6. 06

    Authorized instructions

    The supervisor edits and sends the final instruction to their organization's teams inside the notice radius.

AUTOMATIC / DETERMINISTIC

Alerts act on rules.

Qualifying evidence can raise an alert immediately. Acknowledging it records receipt; it does not clear the condition. Lightning restrikes reset the watch window.

Observation → rule → alert

FIELD / HUMAN REVIEW

A report starts a review.

A crew report marked present can send a potential-hazard notice. Its classification and instructions require the appropriate supervisor's confirmation; an absent report follows its own organization review.

Report → potential notice → confirmation

RESEARCH / ADVISORY

Predictions retain uncertainty.

A confirmed active event, trusted start and qualified site relationship can support a duration estimate. Unsupported categories and insufficient evidence produce unavailable outlooks.

Confirmed event → gated model → advisory

HUMAN APPROVAL BOUNDARY

Gemini can suggest.
A supervisor decides what to send.

Structured AI output supports review. It cannot confirm a hazard, issue a supervisor's instruction, change a score or clear an alert. Deterministic alerts do not wait for this review. Official orders and employer procedures remain separate.

RELIABILITY BY DESIGN

KEEP THE EVIDENCE VISIBLE.

A failed feed, an unreviewed report or a missing artifact should have an explicit outcome. The platform is designed to preserve those distinctions.

01 / ACCESS

Permissions live on the server.

Auth0 identity, roles and memberships protect requests even when someone bypasses the interface.

02 / PROVENANCE

A reading keeps its source.

Per-site evidence retains timestamps, location and source state, so observations and forecasts can be interpreted in context.

03 / FAILURE HANDLING

Missing evidence remains visible.

Per-feed caching marks old evidence stale, then unavailable. API errors remain errors; live requests do not silently become fixtures.

ObservedStaleUnavailable
04 / VERIFICATION

Interfaces are checked.

Regression tests cover contracts, permissions, freshness, deterministic alerts, report review and safe model failures. Research exports preserve provenance and reproducibility.

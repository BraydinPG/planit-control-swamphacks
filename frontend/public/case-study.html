06 / PREDICTIVE CONTEXT, SEPARATE AUTHORITY

Plan for disruption.
Keep uncertainty in view.

For qualified, confirmed active events, FORESITE's historically evaluated duration models estimate recorded-event persistence and remaining duration. That advisory is separate from the hazard index and stop-work state.

07 / COORDINATED RESPONSE

Different roles.
One shared picture.

The crew sees the alert and approved wildlife instruction. The supervisor sees the evidence and pending report. Environmental context, human review and research retain their own meaning.

CREW

Understand the situation.

  • Read assigned site conditions and alerts.
  • Follow supervisor-approved instructions.
  • Submit field evidence when authenticated.
SUPERVISOR

Review and coordinate.

  • Inspect source and report evidence.
  • Confirm applicable hazards within authorization.
  • Review, edit and send instructions.

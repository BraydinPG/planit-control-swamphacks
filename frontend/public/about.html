CONNECTED INTELLIGENCE / PEOPLE IN CONTROL

THE QUESTION THAT STARTED IT

MORE INFORMATION.
A CLEARER PICTURE.

For a utility crew, a regional warning and an on-site hazard are different kinds of information. A forecast cannot inspect a downed line. A report needs human review. A model estimate needs a clearly defined target.

Built for SwampHacks 2026, FORESITE brings those distinctions into one connected workflow: source-qualified environmental evidence, explainable deterministic hazard evaluation, conditional event-duration research, and crew reports reviewed by supervisors.

Our approach pairs typed interfaces and reproducible research with an application that makes sources, uncertainty and responsibility visible. The goal is better-informed preparation and coordination.

OUR ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

THREE PRINCIPLES.
EVERY LAYER.

01

Evidence over assumptions.

Environmental observations and model outputs retain their source, timestamp and uncertainty. Missing information stays visible.

02

Prediction without false certainty.

Historical models support estimates about certain already-active events. They cannot declare a worksite safe.

03

People remain in control.

Field crews supply local evidence. Authorized supervisors confirm applicable hazards and approve instructions.

SAFETY & TRANSPARENCY

INFORMED DECISIONS.
CLEAR RESPONSIBILITIES.

FORESITE provides decision-support context. It does not issue independent work clearance.

01

Know what the information represents.

Live observations, issued forecasts and simulated demonstrations have different meanings. Historical research and replay examples are not current forecasts for a worksite.

02

Keep gaps visible.

Stale or missing readings do not establish safe conditions. Mapped reference locations are not proof of active work orders or complete live sensor coverage.

03

Keep AI within its role.

Gemini can analyze supplied evidence and suggest structured information. Human review confirms classifications and approves instructions; AI cannot change a score or clear an alert.

04

Respect operational authority.

Deterministic rules control application alerts. Official orders, qualified inspection and employer procedures remain separate from model estimates and alert acknowledgements.

PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING / TECHNICAL OVERVIEW

UNDERSTAND THE
INFORMATION FLOW.

These descriptions reflect the implemented application. They are not a legally complete privacy policy or terms of service.

IDENTITY → AUTHORIZED REQUEST

Auth0 and access scope

Auth0 supplies identity tokens. The backend validates requests and applies permissions and organization/team memberships. Frontend role guards improve navigation; they do not replace server checks.

REPORT → STORED EVIDENCE

Tiger Data and worksite records

The application stores sites, observations, reports, reviews, instructions and related state in Tiger Data. Crew reports can include a category, present-or-absent answer, note, location and optional photo reference.

PHOTO → AUTHORIZED VIEW

Private photo storage

Configured production storage uses private Vultr objects; development can use a private local directory. Report-photo routes check the caller's visibility before returning an image.

SUPPLIED CONTENT → AI SUGGESTION

Gemini-supported analysis

Report analysis can send a photo, category, present-or-absent answer, note and selected site context to Gemini. The report's location is excluded from that analysis payload. Generated analysis stays a reviewable suggestion.

For team review

Formal privacy and terms documents, retention commitments and provider/data-sharing disclosures require the team's review before publication. No retention period, legal certification or guarantee is asserted here.
